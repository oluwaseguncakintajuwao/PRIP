# Connecting Skills to PRIP Project Phases

## Portfolio Risk Intelligence Project (PRIP)

The Portfolio Risk Intelligence Project (PRIP) is designed as an end-to-end investment analytics platform rather than a standalone portfolio analysis exercise.

Each project phase develops a specific professional capability while progressively integrating data engineering, quantitative finance, investment research, portfolio construction, automation, business intelligence, DevOps/DataOps, and cloud engineering.

---

## PRIP Skills Mapping

| Phase | Primary Skill Area | Main Skills Demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1a – Portfolio Data Foundation** | **Data Engineering** | API integration, Tiingo market data acquisition, Python, pandas, ETL, data cleaning, schema validation, data-quality controls, environment variables, file management, logging, run metadata, reproducibility, structured project architecture, SQL-ready data design |
| **Phase 1a.5 – DevOps / DataOps Foundation** | **DevOps, DataOps & Software Engineering Practices** | Git, GitHub, version control, `.gitignore`, dependency management, virtual environments, secrets management, automated validation, pytest, GitHub Actions, CI, code-quality controls, reproducible workflows, pipeline logging and documentation |
| **Phase 1b – Performance & Risk Analytics** | **Quantitative & Portfolio Analytics** | Daily returns, cumulative returns, portfolio growth, annualized returns, volatility, covariance, correlation, drawdown, risk-adjusted measures, portfolio mathematics, time-series analysis, reconciliation, statistical validation |
| **Phase 1c – ETF Due Diligence** | **Investment Research & Fund Analysis** | ETF screening, Efficiency–Tradability–Fit analysis, expense analysis, liquidity assessment, tracking characteristics, fund structure, benchmark evaluation, issuer analysis, comparative research and investment due diligence |
| **Phase 1d – Portfolio Rebalancing Engine** | **Portfolio Construction & Automation** | Target weights, current weights, drift analysis, allocation calculations, trade sizing, share calculations, buy/sell recommendations, rebalancing rules, portfolio constraints, scenario analysis, Python automation and Alteryx workflows |
| **Phase 1e – ETF Holdings Overlap Analyzer** | **Data Integration & Exposure Analytics** | ETF holdings ingestion, ticker standardization, company-name matching, data joins, overlap calculations, concentration analysis, common holdings analysis, hidden exposure identification, Alteryx Analytical Apps and parameterized workflows |
| **Phase 1f – Investment Reporting & Decision Support** | **Business Intelligence & Data Visualization** | Power BI, Power Query, DAX, dimensional modelling, KPI development, interactive dashboards, portfolio reporting, risk visualization, investment storytelling and decision-support analytics |
| **Cloud & Deployment Workstream** | **Cloud Engineering & Infrastructure as Code** | AWS, S3, Glue, Athena, Redshift, IAM, Terraform, infrastructure as code, cloud data architecture, automated deployment, GitHub Actions integration and environment configuration |
| **Phase 2 – ETF Premium/Discount Monitoring** | **Market Data, Risk Monitoring & DataOps** | ETF market price vs NAV analysis, premium/discount calculations, tracking difference, tracking error, anomaly detection, underlying holdings data, time-series monitoring, automated pipeline execution, exception reporting and alerts |
| **Phase 3 – ETF Market Mechanics** | **ETF Market Structure & Advanced Investment Analytics** | Creation/redemption mechanism, authorized participants, ETF arbitrage, implied liquidity, bid-ask spreads, market impact, cash drag, sampling, securities lending, tracking efficiency and ETF microstructure |

# Professional Capability Demonstrated

PRIP is designed to demonstrate the ability to move through the complete analytics lifecycle:

> **Acquire → Validate → Transform → Analyze → Model → Automate → Visualize → Deploy → Monitor → Improve**

This means PRIP demonstrates capabilities across several professional disciplines rather than being limited to investment analysis alone:

- **Data Analyst**
- **Financial Analyst**
- **Portfolio Analyst**
- **Investment Analyst**
- **Data Engineer**
- **BI Developer**
- **Analytics Engineer**
- **DataOps Practitioner**
- **DevOps Practitioner**
- **Cloud Analytics Practitioner**

The strength of PRIP is therefore not only the individual calculations or dashboards, but the integration of investment-domain knowledge with modern analytics engineering practices.
